## 1. Your First Words: `console.log()` 🗣️

`console.log()` prints a message to the **console** (the developer's screen). It's the most important debugging tool you'll ever use.

<CodeBlock
  fileName="first-words.js"
  code={`// This is how you talk to the machine
console.log("Hello, World!");
console.log("أهلاً بالعالم!");`}
/>

That's it. Put whatever you want inside the parentheses and quotes, and it shows up on screen.
