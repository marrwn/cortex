### How the Computer Remembers Things — and How You Control That Memory.

---

<AnalogyCard title="The Spice Rack" emoji="🫙" color="#F7DF1E">
  Walk into any Egyptian kitchen. You'll find a **spice rack** with labeled jars:
  **"كمون"** (cumin), **"فلفل"** (pepper), **"كركم"** (turmeric).
  Each jar has a **name** on it and **something inside** it.
  
  A **variable** is exactly this — a **labeled jar** in the computer's memory.
  You choose the label, you choose what goes inside, and you can open it any time to check or change its contents.
